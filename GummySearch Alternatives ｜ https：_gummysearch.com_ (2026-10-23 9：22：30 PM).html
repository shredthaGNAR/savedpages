GummySearch Alternatives

Given that GummySearch is closed as of 11/30/2025 (read more about it here), here's a list of GummySearch alternative products that could help you with your Reddit workflows.

I've seen hundreds of Reddit products pop up in the last few years, and only a few are done well. Here are the ones I recommend. (some of these might be affiliate links)

Cross-Platform Market Research

Use these tools when you want to look beyond Reddit, compare conversations across platforms, and build a broader picture of your market and customers.

  • BuzzAbout: AI social intelligence across Reddit, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X, and LinkedIn. Pull patterns, pain points, and campaign insights from real mentions, with agents and MCP/API for always-on research.
  • Adlicio: Customer research across 30+ platforms. Turn customer comments into ads that convert, inside Claude or any MCP client. (20% discount)

Reddit Lead Generation

Use these tools to spot relevant Reddit conversations, identify people showing buying intent, and respond while the opportunity is still active.

  • RedReach: An AI-based lead monitoring service. Build by another indie maker, check it out!
  • ReplyAgent: AI agent that scans Reddit for relevant conversations and promotes your product.
  • SubredditSignals: Reddit lead-generation tool with buyer intent classification.

Pain Point Analysis

Use these tools to find recurring frustrations, validate that a problem is worth solving, and discover business or product ideas backed by real conversations.

  • PainOnSocial: Find customer pain points and validated business opportunities from Reddit.
  • IdeaFast: Discover recurring customer pain points from Reddit, see the evidence, and turn it into startup ideas.

Reddit posting insights & scheduling

Use these tools to plan what to post, publish at the right time, and manage your Reddit content and engagement from one place.

  • Postpone: The All-In-One Social Media Growth Toolkit. Postpone is the all-in-one platform to create, publish, analyze, and engage everywhere your audience is.
