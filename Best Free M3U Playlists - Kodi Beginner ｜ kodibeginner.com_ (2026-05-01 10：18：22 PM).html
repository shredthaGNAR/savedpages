Best Free M3U Playlists

M3U is a file format that defines multimedia playlists, pointing to the locations of various media streams. By loading the playlists, you can watch free IPTV with thousands of live channels from all around the world.

There are different apps for loading M3U playlists. From those apps, we recommend using Kodi or IPTV app. Other reliable choices include TiviMate, IPTV Smarters, and VLC. Moreover, to exploit the full effectiveness of the playlists, you can even create your own playlist.

We have collected a curated list of the best free M3U playlists, including a selection of free EPGs (Electronic Programming Guides). Additionally, this list is updated regularly, so keep following us for the latest updates.

One helpful addition is to use an EPG (Electronic Program Guide) files with your M3U playlists. It enhances your viewing experience, especially when streaming live TV via IPTV apps like TiviMate, IPTV Smarters, Kodi, or VLC, showing accurate program schedules and channel information.

All the EPGs in this collection are updated daily, so you’ll always have the most accurate and up-to-date program information for your channels.

How to use it: In your IPTV app, go to the settings and look for an option to add an EPG URL. Paste one or more of the addresses below, save the settings, and refresh your channels list to load the guide.
