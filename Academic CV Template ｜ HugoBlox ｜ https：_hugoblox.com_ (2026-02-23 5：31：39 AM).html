The Academic CV Template

The Future-Proof Standard for Modern Academics

Upgrade your online presence with the Academic CV Template—the all-in-one professional portfolio designed specifically for researchers, professors, and PhD candidates.

Built on the powerful HugoBlox ecosystem, this template replaces outdated, static websites with a dynamic, content-rich platform that grows with your career. Join 250,000+ academics who trust HugoBlox to showcase their work.

🚀 Why This Template?

  • 🎓 Purpose-Built for Academia: Pre-configured sections for Biography, Publications, Talks, Projects, Courses, and Lab/Group info.
  • ✨ Stunning & Professional: Clean, modern aesthetics with automatic Light/Dark mode, ensuring your research looks great on any device.
  • 🔒 No Vendor Lock-in: Your content belongs to you. Written in open standards (Markdown/YAML), not trapped in proprietary platforms.
  • ⚡ Blazing Fast: Static site generation means instant load times and perfect SEO, helping your papers get discovered.

🌟 Killer Features

  • 📝 Markdown Slides (New!): Create beautiful, interactive presentation slides directly in Markdown.

    • Future-Proof: No more broken PowerPoint formatting. Write once, present everywhere.
    • Rich Media: Embed local videos, YouTube, Bilibili, and audio seamlessly.
    • Lecturer Friendly: Includes Speaker Notes, Timer, and Progressive Reveals.
    • Code & Math Ready: Perfect for technical talks with syntax highlighting and LaTeX math support ($E=mc^2$).
    • Diagrams: Generate complex flowcharts and diagrams automatically with Mermaid.js.
    • Printable: Export directly to PDF for handouts.

  • 🧠 Intelligent Embedding:

    • Jupyter Notebooks: Render your .ipynb data science notebooks directly on your site.
    • Code Syntax Highlighting: Support for Python, R, Julia, C++, and hundreds more.
    • LaTeX Math: Render complex equations beautifully with KaTeX/MathJax.
    • 3rd Party Integrations: Seamlessly embed Hugging Face spaces, GitHub repositories, and interactive maps.

  • 🎨 Customization Made Easy:

    • No Code Required: Extensive configuration options to match your university colors or personal brand.
    • Zero Maintenance: No plugins to update, no security vulnerabilities to patch.
    • Deployment Ready: Deploys in seconds to GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel.

🎯 Perfect For...

  • PhD Students: Build your professional identity before you graduate.
  • Professors & Lecturers: Host your course materials, slides, and syllabus in one place.
  • Research Groups: Showcase your lab's publications, team members, and open positions.
  • Event Speakers: Share your slides and talk details with a persistent, shareable link.
