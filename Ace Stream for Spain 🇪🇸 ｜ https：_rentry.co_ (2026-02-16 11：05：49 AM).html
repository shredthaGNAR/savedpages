▷ Playlists 📋 for IPTV Apps

Optional, but recommended on Android TV, Fire Stick or TV Box for convenience, since on TV the websites are more difficult to use.
IPTV apps allow you to have playlists with the IDs of each channel.

🔸 Playlists with format acestream://<ID>

They require apps that can be configured to play in an external app, which will be Ace Player.

  • . M3U - Recommended to use IPTV Pro, 2
    • New Era - itsssl.com/zWurW
    • 1RFEF / Icastresana - Events - bit.ly/eventospeticiones

🔸 Playlists with format http://127.0.0.1:6878/ace/getstream?id=<ID>

Requires Ace Stream previously opened in the background. Allows you to play in the same IPTV app.

i️ If there are errones or stops during playback, try this first in the options of Ace Stream apps:

  • Clean cache.
  • Increase the size of the buffer (cache). Recommended: Live Buffer = 60, VOD Buffer = 10.
